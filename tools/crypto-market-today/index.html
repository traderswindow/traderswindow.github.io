    How to read the market in two minutes

    • Price and dominance. Is Bitcoin leading or are altcoins? Rising dominance with a falling market usually means money is hiding in Bitcoin.
    • Sentiment. Fear & Greed tells you the mood, but not the next move. In the data since 2018, extreme greed was followed by better 30-day returns than extreme fear.
    • Leverage. High funding plus rising open interest means a crowded, leveraged market. That's when liquidation cascades happen.
    • The calendar. Fed decisions, CPI and the jobs report move Bitcoin within minutes. Know when the next one is before you open a trade.

    Why is crypto down today?

    Check three things on this page. Funding and open interest show whether leveraged longs were being flushed out; Bitcoin dominance shows whether money is leaving altcoins for Bitcoin; and the calendar shows whether a Fed decision, CPI or the jobs report just came out. Most sharp drops line up with one of the three.
