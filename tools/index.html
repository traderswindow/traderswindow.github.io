Free crypto tools

Live market data in your browser: futures positioning from Binance, market sentiment and the dates that move crypto. No account, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Binance funding rates, liveLive funding rates for every Binance USDT perpetual: current rate, annualized, interval and countdown to the next payment, plus a calculator for what funding costs you. Open interest, liveLive open interest for Binance USDT perpetuals: value in dollars, 1-hour and 24-hour change, the trend over time and what each combination of price and open interest means. Long/short ratio, liveLive long/short ratio from Binance Futures for BTC, ETH and any altcoin: all accounts, top traders and taker buy/sell volume, with the trend over time. Crypto Fear & Greed IndexToday's Crypto Fear and Greed Index with yesterday, last week and last month, a one-year chart against the Bitcoin price, and how Bitcoin did after each reading since 2018. Altcoin season indexIs it altcoin season? A live index of how many of the top 50 altcoins beat Bitcoin over the last 90 days, plus Bitcoin dominance and the total crypto market cap. Crypto economic calendarThe scheduled events that move Bitcoin: FOMC rate decisions, US CPI inflation, the jobs report and BTC options expiry, with countdowns in your time zone and a calendar you can subscribe to. Crypto market hoursCrypto trades 24/7, but its volume follows the stock markets. See which sessions are open right now, in your time zone: Tokyo, London, New York and CME Bitcoin futures.
