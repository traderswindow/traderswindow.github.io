    Why these events move crypto

    • FOMC rate decisions. The US Federal Reserve sets interest rates eight times a year. Higher rates make cash more attractive and risky assets like crypto less so; a surprise in the decision, or in the tone of the press conference, can move Bitcoin several percent within minutes.
    • CPI inflation. The monthly consumer price index is the number that most shapes expectations for the Fed's next move. Hotter than expected usually pressures crypto, cooler than expected usually helps.
    • The jobs report. Nonfarm payrolls and the unemployment rate, out on the first Friday of most months, are the other half of the Fed's mandate.
    • Options expiry. On the last Friday of the month, Bitcoin and Ether options on Deribit settle at 08:00 UTC. Big expiries can pull the price toward levels with large open positions in the days before, and release it afterwards.

    Around these events, spreads widen and liquidations cluster, in both directions. Many traders reduce leverage or simply wait for the first move to finish. See also crypto market hours for the daily rhythm and how liquidations cascade.

    Questions

    When is the next FOMC meeting?

    As of 15 Oct 2026, the next FOMC decision is on Wed 28 Oct at 18:00 UTC. The next date and a live countdown are at the top of this page. The Fed meets eight times a year; the rate decision comes at 2:00 pm New York time on the second day, followed by a press conference at 2:30 pm.

    What time is CPI released?

    US CPI comes out at 8:30 am New York time, usually in the second week of the month. That's 12:30 or 13:30 UTC depending on daylight saving time.

    When do Bitcoin options expire?

    Deribit, the largest crypto options exchange, settles its monthly BTC and ETH options on the last Friday of each month at 08:00 UTC. The quarterly expiries in March, June, September and December are the largest.

    Can I add these dates to my calendar?

    Yes. Subscribe to the calendar link on this page in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook and the events appear with the right time for your time zone.
