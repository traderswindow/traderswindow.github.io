How it works

  1. Load every daily close of the coin on Binance since 2017 (or since it was listed).
  2. Describe today's setup with two facts: is the price above or below its 200-day average, and is it up or down over the last 30 days. Trend and momentum are the signals with the most support in research on crypto.
  3. Find every past day with the same setup and count how often the price was higher 7 or 30 days later. Compare it with all days, because some coins rose on most days anyway.
  4. Count separate cases too: days in the same week overlap, so only days at least one period apart count as separate. The reading needs at least 20 of them.
  5. The likely range takes all past moves of the coin, scales them to how volatile it is right now, and keeps the middle 80%.

Everything is calculated in your browser from Binance's public data. Readings use the last completed daily candle (00:00 UTC), so they change once a day.

What it can't do

  • It can't see news, hacks, listings or a Fed surprise. A single event can break any pattern.
  • Patterns from 2017–2021 may not hold today. In studies of recent years, most simple technical rules stopped beating buy-and-hold.
  • "Higher after 7 days" says nothing about how much. A coin can be up 6 times out of 10 and still lose more on the other 4.
