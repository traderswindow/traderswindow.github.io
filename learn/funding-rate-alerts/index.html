How to get an alert when the funding rate flips or spikes

Funding moves slowly, until it doesn't. A flip from positive to negative, or a jump to an extreme, tells you positioning has changed. Here's how to catch it without staring at a chart.

Which funding changes matter

  • A flip in sign. Positive to negative means shorts now pay longs: the crowd has turned bearish on futures. Negative to positive is the reverse.
  • An extreme reading. On most Binance contracts the neutral baseline is about 0.01% per 8 hours. Readings around 0.05% and above mean one side is paying heavily to stay in, which often comes before a sharp move against that side.
  • The time to the next payment. Some traders adjust positions just before settlement to avoid paying funding.

New to funding? Start with what the funding rate is.

Why it's easy to miss

Most charting tools show funding as a line in a separate pane, and exchanges show it deep in the contract details. Neither tells you when it changes. You find out after the fact.

Funding alerts in Traders Window

  1. Add the coins you trade. Funding appears for any coin with a Binance USDT perpetual.
  2. Under each coin, F shows the current rate and a countdown to the next payment. It turns amber when the rate is extreme.
  3. In Settings → Alerts, turn on Funding flips sign. When it flips, the widget pulses, plays its own sound and shows the new rate on the coin's row.
  4. Optional: send the same alert to your phone with ntfy.

Funding describes positioning, not timing. Treat a flip or an extreme as a reason to look closer, not as a trade signal on its own.
