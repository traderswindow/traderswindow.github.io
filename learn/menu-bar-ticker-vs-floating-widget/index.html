Menu bar crypto ticker or floating widget: which one to use

Both keep a price in view while you work. They're good at different things, and the right one depends on how actively you trade.

Menu bar tickers

A menu bar ticker puts a price next to the clock. There are many, several of them free.

  • Good at: staying out of the way, and showing one or two prices at a glance.
  • Limits: very little space, so there's no room for context like funding or liquidations. Many refresh from aggregators every 30 seconds to several minutes. The menu bar hides in full-screen apps.
  • Best for: holders who check prices occasionally.

Floating widgets

A floating widget is a small window that stays on top of your other apps.

  • Good at: showing more per coin, reacting fast, and making alerts impossible to miss because they happen where you're looking.
  • Limits: it takes some screen space, and a poorly made one gets in the way. Most can't stay on top of full-screen apps.
  • Best for: active traders, especially of futures.
